01 / Technical Depth

LiteRT-LM bypasses traditional fread for multi-GB models, opting for mmap (Memory Mapping) to map disk files directly into the virtual address space.

  • Zero-Copy Loading: Only the weight blocks touched during inference are swapped into physical VRAM by the OS, enabling near-instant hot starts.
  • VRAM Sovereignty: Once LoadModel is called, several GBs of VRAM are physically locked and marked as Permanent. The application layer must precisely reserve RHI resources.
